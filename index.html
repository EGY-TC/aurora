Setup guide

From download to kick-off.

Four steps and you are on the pitch. One launcher covers every Aurora server — no extra clients, no manual patches.

  1. 01

    Download the launcher

    Grab the Aurora Launcher for Windows — a single lightweight client for FIFA 15, FIFA 17 and FIFA 21.

  2. 02

    Open the launcher

    Run it and sign in with your Aurora account. Your username, friends list and stats load instantly.

  3. 03

    Pick your game folder

    Point the launcher at your installed FIFA folder. It verifies your files and applies the online patch automatically.

  4. 04

    Play

    Hit Play and jump straight into matchmaking. Average setup time: under five minutes.
